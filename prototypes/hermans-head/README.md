## Story rule

The physical office and apartment scenes contain Herman, Jay, Heddy, Louise, and Mr. Bracken. Animal, Wimp, Angel, and Genius exist only inside Herman's head, shown in the attic-style mind room or in an unmistakable head cutaway.

## Episode concept

Waterton Publishing must repair and fact-check a million-copy New York visitor guide by 5:00 p.m. Heddy tries to control the assignment, Louise supplies reliable research, Jay offers confident distractions, and Mr. Bracken guards the deadline. Inside Herman's head, Genius, Angel, Wimp, and Animal argue over accuracy, compassion, panic, and appetite.

## Soundtrack prototype

The Guess Who's “No Time” is attached to the deadline/race sequence as a **creative-sync reference**. It can play slightly muted beneath movement and conversation, rise during the run back to Waterton, then fade under the closing punchline.

No audio recording is included. Playback requires separate rights verification or replacement with a cleared, commissioned, or public-domain recording.
